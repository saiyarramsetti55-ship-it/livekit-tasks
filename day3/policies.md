# CityCare Clinic Policies

## Cancellation
Please cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment.

## Late cancellation
If you cancel less than 24 hours before, a late cancellation fee may apply.

## Late arrival
You can arrive up to 15 minutes late. After 15 minutes, we may need to reschedule your visit.

## No-show
If you do not come and do not cancel, it is counted as a no-show. Three no-shows can limit online booking.

## Rescheduling
You can change your appointment date or time. Please do it at least 24 hours before.

## Doctors

## Children's doctor
Dr. Anita Rao sees children for check-ups and vaccines.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, debit cards, credit cards, and UPI.

## Insurance
Most major insurance plans are accepted. Please bring your insurance card.

## What to bring
Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and any previous medical reports.

## Blood tests
For some blood tests you may need to avoid food before the visit. The clinic will tell you at booking.

## Parking
Free parking is available behind the building.
